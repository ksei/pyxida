Supporting documents

Add a LinkedIn data export (the ZIP from LinkedIn → Settings → Data privacy → Get a copy of your data), reference letters, diplomas, certificates or past applications. Their text becomes evidence Pyxida can cite, and Codex checks them against your CV for conflicting titles, dates or degrees.

    Career goals & working style

    Used for culture and career-fit scores, motivation in cover letters, and interview preparation. Everything is optional.

    Your writing style

    Pyxida can learn how you write from past applications you add as supporting documents, so drafts and follow-ups sound like you. It describes your style; it does not copy content.

    Public work GitHub, portfolio, publications

    Codex opens these pages with live search and proposes competencies they demonstrate. Nothing is added until you confirm it. LinkedIn pages need a sign-in and are skipped.

    02

    Find jobs

    Current listings in Switzerland, scored against your experience, languages and preferences.

    Evaluate a specific posting URL or pasted text

    Pasted text is checked for hidden or injected instructions before Codex reads it. The evaluation scores skills, experience, culture and career fit, and checks language, work-permit and location gates.

    Search preferences

    All preferences are optional. Listings you dismissed or applied to are skipped. Deep search runs one Codex search per few portals plus employer career pages, so it uses more of your plan. Choose the country and which portals to search in Settings.

    Prepare applications ↗

    These listings fail a hard gate (a language you have not declared, a work-authorization requirement, or a location constraint). Review them if your circumstances have changed.

          ◎

          Search region

          The country Pyxida searches for this profile.

          Open a saved profile or create one in Profile to choose a country and configure portals. Both are saved per profile.

          The country sets which portals are searched, the languages used to search job ads, the work-authorization options and currency on the Jobs page, and the CV and cover-letter conventions. Portals are kept per country, so switching back restores your list.

          ⌕

          Job portals

          Where Pyxida searches, alongside employer career pages.

          Open a profile to configure its portals.

          Codex searches the switched-on portals with live web search, then opens each listing on the employer's or portal's own page. Starred portals are searched first and most thoroughly. A search hint tells Codex how to use a portal, for example "use the Compliance category" or "ads are mostly in German".
